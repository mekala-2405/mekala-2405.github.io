Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 2026

This is a personal portfolio website. It does not require an account, collect names, or sell any data. It uses privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic — how many people visit, from which countries, and via which referrers.

What is measured

Which analytics tool loads depends on your location, so visitors in stricter privacy regions are never tracked with cookies:

  • Visitors in the EU / EEA and the UK: Cloudflare Web Analytics only. This is cookieless, sets nothing on your device, does not use persistent identifiers, and cannot identify you. No consent is required for this.
  • Visitors elsewhere: Google Analytics 4, which collects aggregate usage data (pages viewed, approximate location, device/browser, referrer) and may use cookies. It is not used to identify you personally.

What is never collected

  • Your name, email, or any personal identity (there is no login or form that stores data).
  • Payment information.
  • Any data sold or shared for advertising by this site.

Your choices

  • You can opt out of Google Analytics with the official browser add-on.
  • Enabling “Do Not Track” or using a content blocker will stop most analytics from loading.
  • California residents may request that personal information not be sold or shared; this site does neither.
